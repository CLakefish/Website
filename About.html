About Me

I'm Carson Lakefish, a 20-year-old Game Designer and Programmer from Duvall, Washington. I've spent the past three years shipping games on indie teams and solo projects, and I'm currently pursuing a bachelors degree in Computer Science and Real Time Interactive Simulation at DigiPen Institute of Technology.

By trade I'm a graphics and engine programmer, but it's design that drives me. I care most about how a game feels, it's accessibility, and experimentation across all fronts. Programming just lets me accomplish what I want to design, it allows me to fill whatever gaps the team needs to succeed; the harder the problem the better, and I never stop iterating.
